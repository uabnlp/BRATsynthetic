## CRITICAL BUGS

1. **MAJOR: Inverted Markov Chain Logic (bratsynthetic/maker/Maker.py:61)**
   - Current code: `do_transition = self.random.uniform(0, 1) >= transition_probability`
   - Problem: Logic is backwards - higher transition probabilities cause LESS transitioning
   - Impact: Fundamentally breaks the Markov strategy that the paper's claims are based on
   - Fix: Change >= to < in the comparison

2. **Syntax Error in Seed Generation (bratsynthetic/BratSyntheticConfig.py:31)**
